Purpose

Producer Publisher is a privately operated tool used to upload and publish approved videos to Facebook Pages managed by its authorized operator.

Information processed

The Facebook publishing integration processes:

  • Facebook Page identifiers, names, and granted management permissions.
  • Access tokens used to authorize Facebook API requests.
  • Video files, titles, descriptions, hashtags, and publication schedules.
  • Publication records, including video identifiers, links, timestamps, and error messages.
  • Information voluntarily provided in support or deletion requests.

How information is used

This information is used to connect authorized Facebook Pages, publish or schedule approved content, prevent duplicate uploads, troubleshoot failures, and respond to requests.

Storage and sharing

Connection settings, access tokens, and publication records are stored on the operator's computer. Video content and associated publishing information are transmitted to Meta to perform the requested publication.

Access tokens are confidential credentials and are not intended for public disclosure. Information is not sold or used for advertising targeting.

Meta processes information received through its services under its own privacy policy.

Retention

Connection information is retained while the integration remains configured. Content and publication records are retained for operation and troubleshooting until removed by the operator or in response to an applicable deletion request.

Data deletion and access removal

To request access, correction, or deletion of information held by Producer Publisher, email adrian.gzz.escalante@hotmail.com with the subject "Producer Publisher Data Request." Identify the relevant Facebook Page and describe your request. Do not include passwords or access tokens.

We may request information needed to verify your authority over the relevant data before acting on the request.

You may also revoke the app's permissions through Facebook's app or business integration settings. Revoking access prevents further authorized access but does not automatically delete locally stored records or previously published Facebook content.

Deleting local records does not delete posts from Facebook. Published content can be managed through Facebook or Meta Business Suite.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated when the integration's practices change. The revised policy will show a new effective date.
